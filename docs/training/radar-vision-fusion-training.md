# EnerTraffic Radar‑Vision Fusion Sensor Training Material

## Overview
This training document covers EnerTraffic roadside millimeter‑wave radar and vision fusion sensor, designed for intelligent transportation perception projects.

Target audiences: field deployment engineers, system integrators, and technical teams working on smart‑traffic infrastructure.

Millimeter‑wave radar delivers stable speed & distance measurement under rain, fog and night‑time conditions. High‑definition cameras provide rich semantic visual information. By spatial‑temporal fusion of both sensor data, the system achieves reliable all‑weather traffic perception for road intersections and highway scenarios.

## Document Content Highlights
The full PDF training material contains seven major chapters:

1. **Challenges & Evolution of Transportation Management**
    - Pain points of traditional single‑sensor traffic monitoring
    - Evolution trend: from single sensing to multi‑sensor fusion & road‑network collaborative perception
2. **Radar‑Video Fusion Technology Brief**
    - Working principles & pros/cons of millimeter‑wave radar and optical video
    - Complementary characteristics of radar and vision for traffic perception
3. **Smart Radar Core Functionalities**
    - Hardware composition: millimeter‑wave radar + multi‑camera array + embedded AI processing unit
    - Multi‑dimension traffic data output: flow rate, average speed, queue length, time‑space headway, vehicle classification, LPR
    - All‑weather perception, target tracking, radar‑video data fusion & visualization
4. **Real‑World Application Scenarios**
    - Incident detection & automatic alert
    - Adaptive intelligent traffic light scheduling
    - Traffic flow statistics & analysis
    - Radar‑triggered video evidence capturing for violations
    - Ramp metering & merge‑area early warning
    - Traffic monitoring under adverse weather (fog, heavy‑rain, night)
5. **Field Deployment Guide**
    - Site selection, installation requirements, power & network specifications
    - Software operation guide for RadarViewer V1.0
    - IP modification, lane configuration, radar‑camera spatial calibration
    - Device OTA firmware upgrade workflow
6. **Device Specifications & Parameters**
    - Electrical, mechanical and environmental specifications
    - Millimeter‑wave radar performance parameters
    - Multi‑camera hardware parameters
7. **Equipment Maintenance & Troubleshooting Tips**
    - Routine maintenance checklist
    - Common fault diagnosis: no data output, trajectory drift, AI recognition degradation
